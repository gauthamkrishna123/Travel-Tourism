Kovalam Beach

Kovalam Beach is a world-famous coastal destination in Kerala, India, located about 16 kilometers south of Thiruvananthapuram.

Munnar

Munnar is a famous hill station in the Idukki district of Kerala, India, sitting 1,600 meters above sea level in the Western Ghats.

the largest waterfall in Kerala, India, standing about 80 to 82 feet tall on the Chalakudy River.
